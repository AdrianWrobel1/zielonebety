8. IMPORTANT METRIC SEMANTICS
==================================================

There are three different concepts:

1. odds_difference

best_odds - lower_odds

2. relative_price_difference_pct

((best_odds / lower_odds) - 1) * 100

3. implied_probability_difference

(1 / lower_odds - 1 / best_odds) * 100

Do not confuse them.

The PRIMARY ranking metric for Quote Discrepancy is:

relative_price_difference_pct

because it expresses the proportional improvement in executable price.

Example:

Betclic = 3.75
Superbet = 2.45

odds_difference = +1.30

relative discrepancy = +53.1%

The UI may display both:

+53.1% DISC
Δ +1.30

but ranking is based on +53.1%.
